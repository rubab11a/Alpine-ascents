Shelter in the Mountains

A Safe Place Above The Clouds

Sheltering is an important part of every mountain expedition. A good shelter provides climbers with a protected place to rest, recover and prepare for the next stage.

From temporary expedition tents to mountain huts and organized base camps, different shelters are used according to the location, weather and type of expedition.

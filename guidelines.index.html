ESSENTIAL RULES

Mountain Safety Guidelines

Follow these essential practices to make your mountain journey safer and better prepared.

01

Plan Your Route

Study your route before starting. Understand the terrain, distance and important points along the journey.

PLAN AHEAD
02

Check Weather

Check the weather before your climb and remain aware of changing conditions throughout the journey.

WATCH CONDITIONS
03

Carry Equipment

Take suitable clothing, food, water, navigation tools and other essential mountain equipment.

PACK SMART
04

Stay With Your Team

Stay connected with your climbing partners and communicate clearly throughout the expedition.

STAY TOGETHER
05

Know Your Direction

Use maps, compass and navigation tools carefully so you can understand your location and route.

STAY ON COURSE
06

Know Your Limits

Pay attention to your physical condition and make responsible decisions when conditions become difficult.

BEFORE YOU CLIMB

Your Mountain Checklist

Before beginning your adventure, make sure you have considered these important preparation points.

Check Your Equipment

Make sure your essential equipment is ready and suitable for the journey.

Review The Route

Understand your planned route and the terrain you may encounter.

Check Weather Conditions

Review the forecast and remain ready for changing conditions.

Inform Someone

Let someone know about your planned route and expected journey.

CLIMB WITH RESPECT

Leave No Trace. Respect The Mountains.

Protect mountain environments by respecting nature, local communities and fellow climbers. A responsible climber helps preserve the mountains for future generations.
